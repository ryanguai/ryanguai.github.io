What didn't work and how I fixed it
Infinite loop in chunker. Section-aware chunking hit 100% CPU on large papers — the sentence splitter's overlap logic didn't advance when overlap exceeded chunk size. Fixed with a minimum-advance guard.
Vector search lacked diversity. Top-10 results clustered around one sub-topic (e.g., 3 chunks from the same paper). Adding BM25 keyword search with Reciprocal Rank Fusion brought in results from different papers and research angles.
